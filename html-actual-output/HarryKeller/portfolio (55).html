Training Philosophy

I've spent the last two years designing and delivering training for a real organization — not as a practicum, but as my job. What keeps me in this field is the moment when someone's performance visibly improves because the instruction was built right.

Good instructional design starts with understanding the learner before writing a single objective. I've been in that seat. I know what it feels like when training clicks — and when it doesn't. I build from that understanding outward.

I believe effective training is the convergence of clear communication, deliberate pedagogy, and a genuine investment in the person being developed. Evaluation isn't the end of the process — it's the beginning of the next iteration.
