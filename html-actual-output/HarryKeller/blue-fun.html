About Me

The full cycle — from learner to designer to supervisor.

I've spent the last two years designing and delivering training for a real organization — not as a practicum, but as my job. What keeps me in this field is the moment when someone's performance visibly improves because the instruction was built right.

I started as a classroom teacher at MTC and within a year was promoted to design the curriculum other teachers learn from. That arc — practitioner to designer to supervisor — is what I bring: I understand the learner's side of the room because I've been there, and I understand the designer's side because it's now my job.

Every role I've held has required me to read a room, communicate clearly, and help people improve. That's the skill I'm building a career on.

Design for performance, not completion

The goal of any training isn't a checked box — it's a measurable change in how someone does their job. I design backwards from the behavior I want to see.

🔄

Iterate with feedback

I've coached teachers individually, gathered performance data, and revised curriculum accordingly. Instructional design is a loop, not a deliverable.

🤝

Meet learners where they are

From small mentoring sessions to large group trainings, I adapt my approach — because adult learners don't respond to one-size-fits-all delivery.
