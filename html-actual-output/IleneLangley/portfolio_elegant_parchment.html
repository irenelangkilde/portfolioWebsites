About Ilene

📦 Large-scale language data pipelines
🏷️ Annotation automation & semi-supervised ML
🔬 NLP modeling from data to deployment
🧪 Independent research with verifiable outputs

I've spent two decades working with language data at every level — cleaning and preparing terabyte-scale corpora, designing annotation pipelines, building the models that learn from that data, and shipping the systems that run in production.

My PhD is in Computer Science from USC, where my dissertation explored automatic sentence generation using hybrid statistical models of lexical collocations and syntactic relations. My independent research period produced novel annotation automation techniques: a novel smoothing approach for POS tagging and dependency parsing, and a framework for interactive ML. My recent work includes training probabilistic word embeddings on 2.4 trillion tokens of parsed web-crawled text.

I bring both the technical depth to understand what the data needs and the engineering experience to build the infrastructure that manages it. My background spans the full NLP stack — linguistic theory, statistical modeling, conversational AI architecture, and the DevOps that keeps it running.
