About

I've been working in NLP since before the term "large language model" existed.

My career started with a PhD thesis on hybrid statistical sentence generation, continued through production conversational AI systems and speech recognition engineering, and now includes building AI-powered tools as an independent developer. What drives me has always been the same: making machines handle language the way people actually use it.

I don't just apply existing NLP tools — I invent techniques. My doctoral work earned an ACL Test of Time Award 25 years later. I hold a patent in NLP task classification. I've trained language models on 2.4 trillion tokens, architected dialog managers from scratch, and shipped production C++ ASR engines. I've also taught the next generation of NLP engineers as an assistant professor at BYU.

Most recently I founded Irene's Ventures, building an AI-powered resume-to-portfolio-website generator — an applied demonstration of where classical NLP depth meets modern generative AI.
