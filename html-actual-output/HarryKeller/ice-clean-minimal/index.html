About

From the classroom to the design desk

I've spent the last two years designing and delivering training for a real organization — not as a practicum, but as my job. What keeps me in this field is the moment when someone's performance visibly improves because the instruction was built right.

I started as a classroom teacher at MTC and within a year was promoted to design the curriculum other teachers learn from. That arc — practitioner to designer to supervisor — defines what I bring. I understand the learner's side of the room because I've been there, and I understand the designer's side because it's now my job.
