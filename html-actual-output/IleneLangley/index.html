I built my first natural language parser before most people had heard the term "machine learning." My career has moved between foundational NLP research, production ASR systems, conversational AI startups, and independent applied research — and the thread through all of it is the same question: how do we get machines to handle language the way people do?

My Ph.D. thesis tackled sentence generation statistically. My patent addressed error prediction in NLP classification systems. My work at Aktify pushed word embeddings to 2.4 trillion tokens. At Lifepod I architected a production dialog manager with patentable innovations, and at Cobalt I built a C++ extension of the Kaldi ASR engine for real deployment.

I design novel algorithms and I ship the code. The ACL recognized one of my 1998 papers with a Test of Time Award in 2023 — evidence that the research holds up across a quarter century of the field's evolution.
