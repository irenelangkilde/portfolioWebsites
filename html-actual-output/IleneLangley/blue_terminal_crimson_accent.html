About Ilene

I've spent two decades working with language data at every level — cleaning and preparing terabyte-scale corpora, designing annotation pipelines, building the models that learn from that data, and shipping the systems that run in production.

My PhD is in Computer Science (NLP focus) from USC. My independent research produced novel annotation automation techniques: a novel smoothing technique for p-o-s tagging and dependency parsing, and a framework for interactive ML. My recent production work includes training word embeddings on 2.4 trillion tokens of web-crawled text at Aktify and building large-scale data preparation pipelines for speech recognition at Cobalt Speech and Language.

Managing language data well is not incidental to my work — it has been central to it. I bring both the technical depth to understand what the data needs and the engineering experience to build the infrastructure that manages it.
